Can You Answer Without the Question?

A common claim in AI research is: if a model answers correctly without seeing the question, it must have memorized the answer during training. But is that really true? Many multiple-choice answers are self-evident — no question needed.

The Hypothesis

Seeing only the answer options is often enough to pick the right one — without any question context. This means high "blind" accuracy is not necessarily evidence of data contamination.

How it works
